## 1. Overview

Dental crowns and bridges are fixed prosthodontic restorations engineered to rebuild both the function and natural aesthetics of your smile.

### Dental Crown (Tooth Cap)
A custom-fitted cap designed to cover, protect and strengthen a single damaged, cracked or root-canal-treated tooth. It preserves your natural root structure while fully restoring chewing ability and shape.

### Dental Bridge
A fixed prosthetic appliance used to replace one or multiple missing teeth in a row. It bridges the gap by anchoring securely onto neighboring natural teeth or dental implants.

### Advanced Materials Offered
* **Monolithic Zirconia:** Delivers maximum mechanical strength for molars subject to heavy biting forces.
* **E-Max (Lithium Disilicate):** Premium all-ceramic choice offering superior translucency and unmatched aesthetic beauty for front teeth.
* **Layered Zirconia:** Combines the high structural strength of a Zirconia core with a natural, tooth-colored porcelain exterior.

## 3. Symptoms & Indications

You may require a dental crown or bridge if you experience any of the following conditions:

### Indications for Dental Crowns
* Weakened, fragile tooth structure following a **Root Canal Treatment (RCT)**.
* Severely fractured, cracked or chipped teeth.
* Large, failing fillings with minimal natural tooth remaining.
* Deep intrinsic discoloration or misshapen tooth structures.

### Indications for Dental Bridges
* One or more missing contiguous teeth creating visible gaps in your smile.
* Difficulty chewing food comfortably or noticeable changes in your bite pattern.
* Facial sagging or jaw stress caused by long-standing tooth loss.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### Q1: What is the difference between a Crown, a Bridge, and a Dental Implant—and which is best for me?
* **Dental Crown:** Covers and protects a single damaged or root-canal-treated tooth when the root is still healthy and intact.
* **Dental Bridge:** Suspends a custom replacement tooth across a missing space, anchored by adjacent natural teeth.
* **Dental Implant:** Surgically replaces a missing tooth root directly in the jawbone without modifying neighboring healthy teeth.

*At **Jerush Dentofacial and Cosmetic Laser Centre**, our prosthodontic specialists evaluate your 3D digital scans to recommend the ideal long-term treatment plan for your specific case.*

### Q2: How long do Zirconia and Ceramic crowns or bridges last?
When crafted with high-grade materials like Monolithic Zirconia or E-Max ceramic, restorations typically last **10 to 15+ years**—and often a lifetime with proper oral hygiene and regular checkups.

### Q3: How many days will international patients need to stay for treatment?
Thanks to our digital CAD/CAM workflows and in-house laboratory processing, most crown and bridge treatments are completed in **3 to 5 business days**.

### Q4: Will my new crown or bridge look natural alongside my existing teeth?
Yes. We utilize advanced digital shade-matching technology to match the exact shade, texture and translucency of your adjacent teeth for a completely natural look.

### Q5: Is getting a crown or bridge painful?
No. Tooth preparation is performed under local anesthesia to ensure complete comfort. Post-treatment sensitivity is minimal and resolves within 24 to 48 hours.
