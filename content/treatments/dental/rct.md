What is Root Canal Treatment?



Root canal treatment focuses on doing away with micro organism from an inflamed teeth and preventing reinfection.

When the pulp—the innermost part of the enamel that consists of nerves and blood vessels—becomes infected or damaged past repair, it must be removed.

The complete system of pulp elimination, cleansing, sealing, and restoring the enamel is called RCT (Root Canal Treatment). This conservative approach saves your herbal teeth, which are then reinforced with a filling or dental crown.

Who Might Need a Root Canal Treatment?



Root canal treatment is suggested in case you are experiencing:



Persistent pain or sensitivity to warm and cold

Tooth injuries like cracks or chips that expose the nerve

Severe enamel decay that has reached the pulp

Swelling, abscess, or infection near the root

Desire to maintain your natural enamel and keep away from dentures or implants
