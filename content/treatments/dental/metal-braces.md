## 1. Overview

Traditional metal orthodontic braces remain the most reliable and widely chosen method for correcting misaligned teeth and complex bite issues. Constructed from high-grade stainless steel brackets and archwires, modern metal braces are significantly smaller, lighter and more comfortable than earlier designs.

At **Jerush Dentofacial & Cosmetic Laser Centre**, our specialist orthodontists customize each bracket placement to ensure optimal movement, delivering straight teeth and healthy oral function safely and efficiently.

### Traditional Metal Braces vs. Modern Upgrades
* **Standard Metal Braces:** Uses high-grade metal brackets bound by flexible archwires and colorful elastic tie bands.
* **Self-Ligating Metal Braces:** Utilizes built-in sliding clips instead of rubber bands to hold wires, resulting in fewer friction points and easier cleaning.

## 3. Symptoms & Indications

Metal orthodontic braces effectively address 8 key dental alignment conditions:

* **Crowded Teeth:** Fixes space issues to guide squeezed teeth into alignment[cite: 4].
* **Overlapping Teeth:** Straightens twisted or overlapping teeth for easier cleaning[cite: 4].
* **Teeth Gaps (Diastema):** Closes unwanted spaces between front or side teeth[cite: 4].
* **Overbite:** Reduces excessive upper front tooth overlap over lower teeth[cite: 4].
* **Underbite:** Realigns lower teeth that stick out past upper front teeth[cite: 4].
* **Crossbite:** Fixes upper teeth that sit inside lower teeth when biting[cite: 4].
* **Open Bite:** Closes the gap when upper and lower teeth don't meet[cite: 4].
* **Orthodontic Relapse:** Realigns teeth that shifted back years after wearing braces[cite: 4].
