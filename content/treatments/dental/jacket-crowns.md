**How Treatment for Jacket Crowns Operates:**  
 The visible portion of a tooth is entirely covered by a tooth-shaped cap called a jacket crown. It enhances the size, shape, and color of a tooth while protecting a weak or damaged one. The following procedures are used at Jerush Clinic to deliver the treatment with precision and care:

* **Examining and Consulting**

 To determine whether a jacket crown is the best option for you, our dentist uses digital X-rays or scans to assess the condition of your teeth.

* **Getting Your Teeth Ready**

 To create room for the crown, the damaged tooth is gently shaped. This ensures a perfect fit and natural alignment with your other teeth.

* **Design of the Crown and Impression**

 We take digital scans or impressions of your teeth. Based on your dental structure and choice, our experts design a crown that matches your natural tooth color.

*  **Placement of Temporary Crowns**

 While the permanent crown is being made, a temporary crown is positioned to protect your tooth.

* **Last Crown Repair**

 Our dentist restores your lovely smile by carefully fitting and bonding the custom jacket crown to your tooth when it is ready.
