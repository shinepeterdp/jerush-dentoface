# Clear Aligners

## Straighten Your Teeth Without Metal Braces

A confident smile doesn't have to come with metal brackets and wires. Clear aligners offer a simple, comfortable way to straighten teeth while fitting naturally into your daily life.

Made from transparent, medical-grade material, each set of aligners gently moves your teeth little by little. Since they're removable, you can enjoy your favorite foods, brush, floss, and attend meetings or social events without feeling self-conscious.

At Jerushaligne Clear Aligners, every treatment is planned with precision, helping you achieve a healthier smile with minimal disruption to your routine.

---

## How Clear Aligners Work

### 1. Consultation & Smile Assessment

Your journey starts with a detailed dental examination. We check your teeth, bite, and overall oral health to see if clear aligners are the right choice.

### 2. Digital Scan

A comfortable 3D scan captures the exact shape of your teeth. No messy impression materials or uncomfortable molds.

### 3. Treatment Planning

Using digital technology, every stage of tooth movement is mapped out. You'll even get an idea of how your smile is expected to look once treatment is complete.

### 4. Aligner Fabrication

A series of clear aligners is created based on your treatment plan. Each aligner is designed to continue the movement from where the previous one stopped.

### 5. Wear & Progress

You'll wear each aligner for around 20 to 22 hours every day, changing to the next set as advised. Regular review appointments help us monitor your progress and keep everything on track.

### 6. Retainers

Once your teeth reach their final position, retainers help maintain your new smile for years to come.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does treatment take?

Treatment time depends on the complexity of your case. Some patients finish within six months, while others may require 12 to 18 months.

### How many hours should I wear the aligners?

For the best results, aligners should be worn for 20 to 22 hours every day. Remove them only while eating, drinking hot beverages, brushing, and flossing.

### Are clear aligners painful?

You may notice slight pressure for the first couple of days after switching to a new aligner. This is a normal sign that your teeth are moving.

### Can I eat while wearing aligners?

No. Remove the aligners before eating or drinking anything other than plain water.

### Will people notice I'm wearing aligners?

Most people don't. The transparent material makes them much less noticeable than traditional braces.

### How often are review appointments needed?

Most patients visit every six to eight weeks so we can check progress and provide the next set of aligners if required.

### Can teenagers wear clear aligners?

Yes. Many teenagers are suitable candidates, depending on their dental condition and commitment to wearing the aligners as instructed.

### What happens after treatment?

You'll receive retainers to help keep your teeth in their new position and protect your results.
